# Published timed main-mod semantics

The normal item page's five **effect levels** are separate from the Field Guide's 1–17 **subattribute levels**. The model never maps one to the other from rarity, description count or level number. An exact photographed identity, ordinary/Shiny variant and Field Guide level takes precedence. The curated community candidate in `evidence_packets/published-main-core-v1.json` carries its own item ID, source URL and effect level; it requires the existing community core what-if control.

## Source-text batch

Compare can show the held public main-effect texts for a selected mod. Choosing one text and the existing community-core what-if control applies only that exact text to the selected **ordinary** identity. It does **not** infer an item effect rank from Field Guide level. The named additive-pool model now executes 26 of the 57 normal-source identities: static Crit Rate/Crit DMG, Weapon, Status, Elemental and Weakspot stats; Fortune's Favor's crit/weakspot tradeoff; a named active/inactive Secluded Strike or Targeted Strike condition; whole-meter Distant Strike distance; and Bullet Siphon's previous-bullet magazine counter. The source-text parser rejects text absent from that identity's held variants. An exact photographed identity/level takes precedence over a chosen community text.

The [operator ledger](evidence_packets/public-core-operator-ledger-v1.json) records the original **24** timed/counter/resource identities, **six** HP or melee utility identities, **one** normal identity without effect text, and **seven** Shiny-only identities. Six of the timed identities are now connected to direct-shot timing as described below; the ledger retains its original classification for audit. The selected source text comes from held community detail variants; its normal item ID comes from the Wikily crosswalk. The two are distinct provenance fields, and a variant text is not asserted to have a known Wikily item effect rank. Previewed damage is still conditional and target mitigation is absent.

## Conditional completeness and timed direct hits

The published Lv10 suffix lines are now separated by outgoing-damage dependency. Max HP and incoming-damage reduction are returned as **utility lines**, with their values and source rows retained. They do not block a direct outgoing-damage total when the scenario contains no HP-dependent offensive feedback. Unknown offensive lines still block it.

“Damage against enemies” has a published numeric line but no verified game bucket equation. The guided Compare asks for its activity fraction and an explicit **independent multiplicative target-pool hypothesis**. If a sourced packet occurs, its target scope is a second explicit assumption; an unknown packet target keeps the total partial. The one-second KAM example with the target line active, aerial extra inactive, selected normal source text, and the named bucket hypothesis now yields **21 complete conditional totals out of the 26** arithmetic core identities. It remains a modeled comparison, never a game-verified winner. If target activity or grouping is unknown, the result remains partial. The direct-only published reference explicitly excludes the target line, so its ratio is withheld when the line is active.

Five of the 26 remain partial in that representative setup: Secluded Strike Mirror has an unmapped Firearm Damage line; Fortune's Favor General has a Reload Speed rating without seconds conversion; its two Lunar suffixes have unpublished low-HP magnitude curves; Targeted Strike Bull's Eye has three keyword duration/crit/weakspot interactions without a mapped target effect. These are **one, one, two and one identities**, respectively. Supplying a false condition or treating an unknown value as zero does not clear them.

Six of the formerly 24 timed/resource identities now have executable **direct-shot** source-text scenarios: two Critical Surge variants gain independently expiring Crit DMG stacks after an empty magazine, two Three Strikes variants boost the first three landed shots after a completed reload subject to the half-magazine cap, and two Deadshot variants use explicitly entered crit-hit shot indices to build Crit DMG stacks that reset on reload. The event ordering is post-shot activation and half-open expiry. Their source item effect rank is still separate from Field Guide level, and ordinary community text remains an opt-in. When a sourced packet also occurs, its interaction with these timed core pools stays unresolved rather than applying a static average to every packet.

## Sourced packet and finite-ammo core windows

The [execution ledger](evidence_packets/public-core-execution-v2.json) now classifies the original 24 timed/resource identities by actual operator support: **six direct-shot timeline rules**, **17 sourced-packet timeline rules**, and **one blocked Attack-basis rule**. The 17 newly connected identities are Abnormal Increase (11), Elemental Resonance (2), Precision Charge (2), and Reload Rampage (2). These counts mean the selected source-text operator executes under its required inputs; they do not mean every suffix, source rank, target or full build is known.

Abnormal Increase applies Status DMG to later classified status packets after a magazine empties. If two active stack windows affect the same packet, the user must select an independent-duration hypothesis. Elemental Resonance counts classified elemental damage instances in the preceding magazine and starts its bonus on reload completion. It requires confirmation that the timeline includes all relevant elemental events; overlapping active reload windows require a replacement hypothesis. Precision Charge takes an explicit weakspot-hit count for each completed magazine, checks it against fired shots, and requires a consistent overall direct weakspot share. Its bonus resets on the next reload and applies only to later elemental packets.

Reload Rampage uses explicit zero-based kill shot indices. A kill happens **after** that shot, transfers up to two rounds from finite reserves into the magazine, and grants a nonstacking Weapon/Status bonus on later eligible packets until reload. The half-magazine limit is modeled as a per-trigger cap only when the user chooses that named hypothesis. Kill events with no reserve transfer zero rounds; duplicate event IDs do not refill twice. Shot counts and reload times are regenerated from the resulting ammo state. An Outer Space Power Surge scenario demonstrates the status packet adjustment and a KAM versus Outer Space saved-plan fixture reaches a complete **conditional** timed comparison without mutating a plan.

Most Wanted (one identity) remains blocked: its source text grants **Attack** per marked-enemy event, while the published weapon reference uses a damage-per-projectile baseline. No sourced Attack-to-reference conversion or mark-event times are available in this model. It is not equated with Weapon DMG.

The [Mirror suffix source](https://oncehuman.th.gl/db/mods/mod_9411) components also reveal a base Crit DMG value plus a separate behind-target extra; the engine now keeps the base when behind-target activity is zero. Its Firearm Damage line retains the same sourced base/extra breakdown but remains outside an outgoing damage pool because neither the held source nor official release text establishes its relationship to Weapon DMG. The [Bull's Eye suffix source](https://oncehuman.th.gl/db/mods/mod_9625) lists duration, crit and weakspot magnitudes, but does not establish which effect or marked-target packets they modify. The five original arithmetic blockers therefore remain gated. HP and defensive utility lines stay separate, and an HP-based core still prevents a complete outgoing result when its offensive dependency is unresolved.
