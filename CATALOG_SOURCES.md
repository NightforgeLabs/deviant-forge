# Catalog sources and attribution

## November 1, 2026 current Field Guide gap check

The selectable union has 984 mod identities; 632 are classified as current Field Guide identities. Published fixed four-line profiles cover 630 of those 632 across levels 1–17. The two blocked identities are Shoes `Steady Hand <Resistance>` and `Slow and Steady <Resistance>`. Their [Steady Hand](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods/steady-hand-resistance) and [Slow and Steady](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods/slow-and-steady-resistance-98147b245d78) pages identify the mods and list main-effect ranks, but do not publish the four ordered Field Guide subattributes. No other suffix's layout is transferred to Resistance.

Of the 632 current identities, 568 carry a catalog main-effect text and 64 have no single mapped text. Sixty-three already had identity-linked descriptions in `catalog_details.json`; the remaining `Elemental Resonance <Deviant Energy>` now links by exact [item ID 19500368](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods/elemental-resonance-deviant-energy) in `evidence_packets/public-core-identity-v1.json`. This overlay leaves the frozen main-effect packet's hashed inputs unchanged. The public detail API exposes all 64 descriptions as `unbound_variants` with their exact page URL and the `published_variants_level_unmapped` status; the catalog view shows them without selecting one for the preview level. The descriptions do not establish the 1–17 rank mapping, so the calculation engine does not apply their numeric main effects. A direct current-level tooltip or a published level-to-effect mapping would close that distinct gap. Saved Field Guide and inventory records are untouched.

- [OnceHuman_Tools](https://github.com/lReDragol/OnceHuman_Tools), pinned commit `d6b1d5b99f84cb6ddc6b8f85144ee067af2a4e45`, [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/lReDragol/OnceHuman_Tools/blob/d6b1d5b99f84cb6ddc6b8f85144ee067af2a4e45/LICENSE): community mod, weapon, armor, and set JSON used to generate `catalog.json`. The catalog normalizes names, variants, categories, and explicit uncertainty fields. Community descriptions are treated as historical leads until checked against current game tooltips or official updates.
- [Once Human Database mod index](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods), captured 2026-10-22 in `catalog_current.json`: a current community identity and icon-link snapshot. The page exposed 1,479 rows; 1,473 parsed, six rejected. Duplicate rows collapse by name, variant, and slot. The union with the pinned snapshot contains 957 distinct mod identities, of which 837 appear in this current snapshot. Five main-effect text conflicts are flagged rather than silently overwritten. Linked icon assets remain on the source site; they are not bundled into the app.
- [Once Human Database mod detail pages](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods), captured individually in `catalog_details.json`: 1,473 pages fetched, 1,341 with effect-level descriptions. They supply descriptions for 1,297 index rows with null effects. The page does not identify which listed effect-level description applies to each Field Guide level; all candidates are retained separately.
- [Wikily mod catalog](https://wikily.gg/once-human/mods/), captured in `catalog_systems.json`: 1,825 records classify 1,618 new-system records, 207 legacy records, and 809 Shiny records. Matching is by displayed name and slot and remains community corroboration, not a claim that every listed Shiny is currently obtainable.
- `catalog_numeric.json`: sixteen ordered numeric layouts visually checked against native-resolution crops from the 2026-10-22 user recording. Every value links to its exact image. The [official Field Guide chart](https://www.oncehuman.game/news/devBlog/20251212/40781_1276354.html) maps mod level to ordered attribute tiers and states that new subattributes are fixed, unlike legacy random rolls. The resolver shares a numeric tier only when at least two distinct current cards show the same named attribute, tier, base value, direction, unit, conditions, conditional bonus, activation, stacking, and applicability. Conditional bonuses remain tied to their own variant. No value is interpolated for an unseen tier.
- The [official May 2026 Gravity Abyss preview](https://www.oncehuman.game/news/devBlog/20260522/40781_1301328.html) also contains two pictured Lv. 17 Shiny cards. `research/official_mod_cards.json` records the image hash and exact ordered attributes for Elemental Resonance `<Aero>` and Ferocious Charge `<Downstar>`; `tools/import_official_mod_cards.py` reproducibly imports them and a visually checked same-level Distant Strike main effect. The ordinary Lv. 17 subattributes are shared with Shiny by the official Field Guide rule; pictured Flash bonuses are not assigned to ordinary mods. Complete numeric identity-level coverage is now 32 profiles across 18 identities.
- `catalog_effect_magnitudes.json` is reproducibly generated from downloaded community detail descriptions by `tools/audit_detail_magnitudes.py`. It contains 5,182 numeric main-effect candidates for 706 catalog identities, each marked as **unmapped to Field Guide level**. They are search/reconciliation evidence, not applied effect rules.
- `weapon_capabilities.json`, `weapon_profiles.json`, `armor_capabilities.json`, and `damage_rules.json` record exact-identity mechanic classifications and the narrow set of currently calculable rules. They distinguish historical community, current client-derived community, official update, and native/official card evidence. See [BUILD_DAMAGE_MODEL.md](BUILD_DAMAGE_MODEL.md) for the supported saved-build comparison and its assumptions.
- A [community report of a Lv. 17 Aero comparison sheet](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/once-human-the-gravity-abyss-vision-guide-for-august-2026-crystals-aerial-hunt-and-aero-mods) supplies the top-tier Weapon DMG, Crit DMG, All DMG Boost, and Max HP values for the already photographed Precision Charge <Aero> layout. This is labeled as corroborated community evidence, not official per-entry scaling.

The union is **not** a verified complete list of all current mods. Generic acquisition categories are not item-specific drop paths. The official level chart does not by itself establish an entry's four attributes or numeric values. `catalog_numeric.json` supplies only corroborated values; all other tiers remain unresolved.

- [Official September 2025 Against All Odds update](https://www.oncehuman.game/news/devBlog/20250922/40781_1260687.html) and [official January 2025 Power of Striving preview](https://www.oncehuman.game/news/update/20250110/40780_1205980.html): corroborate their baseline Weapon/Status bonuses and HP/stamina-dependent additions. The exact +10% main effects for Against All Odds `<Precision>` Lv. 7 and Power of Striving `<Downstar Deviant Energy>` Lv. 9 are independently visible on native recording crops `0091.png` and `0094.png` and stored as same-level `verified_main_effect` records in `catalog_numeric.json`. The [Against](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods/against-all-odds-precision) and [Power](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods/power-of-striving-downstar-deviant-energy) detail pages show several effect strengths but do not prove which maps to a Field Guide level. The outgoing example requires the same-level image record before calculating either main effect; see [OUTGOING_MODEL.md](OUTGOING_MODEL.md).
- `artwork_manifest.json` captures local image paths, source image URLs, source pages, exact-name matching method, unresolved ambiguity, and coverage from current [mod](https://www.oncehumandb.com/mods), [weapon](https://www.oncehumandb.com/weapons), and [armor](https://www.oncehumandb.com/armor) listings. `tools/cache_artwork.py` recreates the mapping and cache. Weapon/armor listing artwork is accepted only for a **unique exact displayed-name match** to a catalog identity; ambiguous names remain without artwork. Coverage is 837/957 mods, 121/138 weapons, and 161/162 armor entries cached. This secondary-source artwork is separate from game-mechanics evidence.
- **Family emblem assets (historical and contemporary candidates; current shape checked):** The [MetaForge gold mod guide](https://metaforge.app/once-human/once-human-every-gold-mod) and [OhDex mod catalog](https://ohdex.gg/mod-list) identify complete transparent family images. Four Weapon/Burn and four Weapon/Power Surge symbols were individually compared with the user's 2026 game screenshot/recording before slot-and-family mapping in `static/mod-reference.js`. The local copies are in `static/artwork/`; source URLs are recorded per entry. Same-name historical art for Surge Amplifier, Shock Rampage, and Burning Wrath conflicted with the visible current shapes and was rejected in favor of matching OhDex assets. This establishes eight visual identities only, not current mechanics, ownership, or permission for external redistribution.
- **Tech-level roll activation:** The [official October 2026 Isles of Abyss update](https://www.oncehuman.game/banner/20261021/41906_1317280.html) says active Mod attribute count unlocks gradually with Tech level. Individual active rows and thresholds are not yet mapped for a saved character. Typed roll calculations exclude unknown-active rows by default and permit a separately labeled assume-active what-if; they do not call excluded values zero in game.
