## Product architecture and acceptance

The game catalog (`catalog.json`) is separate from account observations (`observations`), confirmed Field Guide unlocks and crafted equipment (`inventory`), and loadout measurements (`measurements`). A recording supplies identity, visible level, unlock/equip cues, and variable rolls. Catalog lookup supplies known effects and the official level tier profile. Unknown values, activation, and stacking remain explicit. Recognition must never turn a blueprint sighting or a dark/locked card into owned equipment.

Acceptance for a trustworthy recommendation: legal owned or ideal equipment combinations, a confirmed primary weapon base or exact contextual measurement, all applicable effects modeled or explicitly unranked, and a trace showing the relevant source, activation, and uncertainty. A numerical winner is withheld when unresolved gear differences can change the result.
